# Tiny Street — How to Play (Delivery Logic, 0.8.0)

The café storeroom is full of crates. Push each crate onto the pad that shows
**the same picture**. A puzzle is solved when every pad holds its own crate.

## The pieces
| | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Courier** | you; walks one square at a time |
| **Crate** | a square wooden box with a picture: coffee, croissant, strawberry or tea |
| **Pad** | a round mat with the same picture; stays visible under a crate |
| **Wall** | brown blocks; nothing passes through |

## Moving
* **Swipe** on the board, or use the **arrow buttons**, to walk one square.
  On a computer, the arrow keys (or W A S D) work too, and a game pad's
  D-pad does the same. **Z** undoes and **Y** redoes. **Tab** moves between
  buttons, **Enter** or **Space** presses the highlighted one, and **Escape**
  goes back.
* **Tap** an empty square to walk there. Walking never pushes a crate.
* Walk into a crate to **push** it one square. You **can't pull** a crate, and
  you **can't push two** at once.

## Pads
* A crate on a pad with a **different** picture doesn't count. You can push it
  off again.
* Crates stay movable after delivery: sometimes a delivered crate has to step
  aside and come back.

## When a route is blocked
Walls and corners can trap a crate for good. When the game can **prove** that
the puzzle can no longer be finished, a **Route blocked** card says why:
* a crate is stuck where it can never reach a matching pad,
* crates are jammed and can never move again, or
* every possible sequence of pushes from here was checked and none finishes.

Tap **Undo** (free, unlimited) or **Restart**. If the game can't prove it, it
says nothing and lets you keep trying.

## Hints (free)
1. **Idea**: what this puzzle is about.
2. **Strategy**: which crate to work on next, whether it must move away from
   its pad first, and the fewest pushes left from here.
3. **Next push**: an arrow on one crate.

Hints are worked out from your current position. Using one only skips the
"Solved without hints" badge for that attempt.

## Rewards
* The **first** time you solve a puzzle you earn restoration tokens (3–5),
  once. Spend them on **Restore** to bring the café back to life.
* Your **best** pushes and steps are kept for each puzzle.
* **Fewest pushes** medal: your pushes equal the proven minimum (found by
  checking every possible position).
* **Solved without hints** badge.

Pushes are what count; walking steps are shown separately.

## Tips
* Before pushing, ask: *after this, can I still get behind every crate I
  need to move?*
* Pads in a dead end fill from the far end first.
* The nearest delivery isn't always the first one.
