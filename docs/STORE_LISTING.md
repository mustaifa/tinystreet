**App name (≤30):** Tiny Street: Sort & Restore
**Short description (≤80):** Sort deliveries, fill café orders and restore a cosy neighbourhood café.

**Full description:**
> Welcome to Tiny Street, where a forgotten corner café is waiting for someone to bring it back to life.
>
> Each puzzle is a calm little delivery. Tap a crate to send its front item to a waiting customer. If nobody needs it yet, it rests on the counter. Counter space is limited, though, so plan your order of moves.
>
> • 60 hand-checked puzzles, every one verified solvable. No luck and no hidden changes.
> • Two-part orders that reward thinking ahead.
> • No timers, no lives, no energy. Play at your own pace.
> • Free unlimited undo and restart, plus a hint that works from your current position.
> • Earn restoration tokens and rebuild the café step by step: storefront, windows, coffee counter, seating and a grand reopening.
> • Choose wall colours, seating, plants and sign styles.
> • A new daily puzzle, playable offline.
> • English and العربية.
> • Plays fully offline. No account needed.
