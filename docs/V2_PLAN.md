# Tiny Street v2: diagnosis and plan

Written 2026-10-16, after the first real-device smoke test. The owner's report: the APK installs and renders, but the game "feels very basic". The diagnosis below comes from reading the code and the v1 screenshots, not from guesses.

## Why v1 feels basic (from inspection)
| Area | What exists in v1 | Problem |
|---|---|---|
| Core interaction | Tap crate → item auto-routes. Press-and-hold preview, fly arcs, soft shake on invalid. | The one decision (which crate) is invisible: no idle cue shows where each item will go. Feedback is small: no particles, no streaks, no customer reaction. Delivery is a faint tick. |
| Strategic choice | Counter pressure, tray priority, two-stage orders (from L31) | Levels 1–30 are the same puzzle at growing size. No new rule appears for 30 levels. |
| Restoration | 5 milestones, 1 token per first clear, costs 8–16 | The first visible reward comes after **8 levels**, and steps are 10–16 levels apart. The café is an exterior facade, so "counter" and "seating" are tiny details in windows. Choices exist, but the player only finds them inside a list. Stars earn nothing. |
| Personality | Generic coloured person icon on each order | No cast, no story, no reason to care who is being served. |
| Return reasons | Daily puzzle with streak (no reward) | No objectives, achievements or progress view. |
| Difficulty | Generated by target random-win rate | Proxy spikes: c025 (3% random win in a 25% tier), c034 (10%, 3rd level of a new mechanic), c048 (6%), c057 (3%). |
| Visuals | Clean cream UI, good product silhouettes | Flat cream screens and an empty-looking board, with no setting or warmth on the game screen. |
| Audio | Synthesised SFX, short music loop | The loop is short and repetitive. Combo and unlock moments have no distinct sound. |

## Priorities (in order)
1. **A: Core feel.** Idle destination badges on every crate (icon + number, not colour alone); lift-and-guide on press; drag-or-tap; landing sparkles; delivery burst with customer reaction; streak ("combo") feedback with rising pitch; order progress bar; a celebratory finish sequence; café-themed game background.
2. **B: Mechanics** (all in `rules.gd`, all solver-verified):
   - **Fresh items** (`coffee!`): hot and must go straight into an order; never onto the counter. Creates real sequencing choices.
   - **Paper bags** (`?tea`): wrapped items stay hidden until they reach the front. Adds a choice about what to reveal first.
   - **Delivery locks** (`locks`): a crate stays strapped shut until N orders have been served. Forces the player to plan around supply that hasn't arrived yet.
   Each gets a one-line, in-play first-use coach, a forgiving intro level, then gradual combination. The two-stage orders from v1 stay.
3. **C: Restoration.** An interior cut-away café with 8 visible steps (clean, walls, floor, counter, lights, seating, plants, sign and opening). 7 steps offer 3+ visual choices that can be picked at purchase and changed later. Collectible extras (café cat, clock, cake stand, gramophone) give tokens a use after the café is complete. Tokens = best stars per level (idempotent grants), so the first step is affordable after level 1–2.
4. **D: Personality and return.** Cast: Mira (baker next door, mentor), Theo (sleepy student, coffee), Mr. Basil (retired postman, tea). Short skippable lines on chapter, mechanic and restoration beats. Their orders show their faces. Café tasks: 3 a day, local clock, documented. ~16 achievements with token or cosmetic rewards. An optional *Rush* challenge (3 validated daily-pool puzzles, count-up timer, personal best only). A *Journal* progress view.
5. **E: Integration.** Save schema v3 with migration, tests, validator, QA shots, Android build `build/tinystreet-v2-debug.apk`.

## Non-goals
No accounts, backend, leaderboards, multiplayer, forced ads, lives or energy. No timers in the campaign.
